      Words you'll see on this site

      Plain-English glossary
      ADS-B

      A radio signal every modern aircraft broadcasts about twice a second with its position, altitude and speed. Volunteers' receivers pick it up and share it with OpenSky.

      Flight no. vs callsign

      You book "6E 2134". Air traffic control hears "IGO2134". Same flight: the first uses the airline's IATA code, the second its ICAO code.

      On-time (OTP)

      India's DGCA counts a flight as on time if it leaves within 15 minutes of schedule. That's the rule used on this site.

      METAR

      The standard airport weather report, issued about every 30 minutes: temperature, wind, visibility and codes like HZ (haze) or BR (mist).

      VT- registration

      Every Indian-registered aircraft's tail number starts with VT. It identifies the exact aircraft, across all the flights it flies in a day.

      Flight cycle

      One takeoff plus one landing. Engines wear by cycles as much as by hours, so short-haul jets age faster per hour flown.

      Hot-day takeoff

      A takeoff at 30 °C or more (ISA+15). Hot air is thin, so engines run harder. Engineers track these as a wear factor.

      Block time

      Gate to gate: from pushback to arrival at the gate. It's longer than the time in the air because it includes taxiing.

      How SkyStream gets this data

      Every number here comes from a free, public source

      Capture

      Every 5 minutes, Python producers pull aircraft positions from OpenSky and weather from AviationWeather.gov.

      Stream

      Each record goes onto a Kafka topic. Bad records go to a dead-letter topic instead of breaking the pipeline.

      Process

      Spark Structured Streaming cleans positions, detects takeoffs and landings, and joins them with airport weather.

      Serve

      Results are stored in Delta Lake tables and served to this dashboard through a small API.
